# HEXFALL Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

HEXFALL ("the game") is a single-player virtual reality game for Meta Quest made by RedMan Studios ("we", "us").

## What the game collects

Nothing about you. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no online features. It does not connect to the internet, and it does not ask for access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts or files.

## What the game stores on your headset

The game keeps one save file, plus a backup copy of it, on your Meta Quest headset. It holds:

- your progress: runs played, best floor, Hex Shards, upgrades, achievements and story progress
- your settings: comfort, hand, menu size, controls and story options

These files stay on your headset. We never receive them and cannot see them.

## Meta

You buy and run the game through Meta's Horizon Store and Horizon OS. Meta collects information about your account, purchases, device and app usage under its own privacy policy: <https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/>. The game is built with Meta's software development kit, which may send technical information about how the kit is used to Meta. We do not receive personal data about you from Meta.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game from your headset permanently deletes its save files. We hold no other copy, so there is nothing else to delete. Questions about data Meta holds go to Meta through the link above.

## Changes

If a future update adds a feature that collects information, we will update this policy before that update is released and change the date above.
