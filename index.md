# HEXFALL

A physics-magic roguelite for Meta Quest 3 and 3S.

Hurl fireballs, freeze enemies and shatter them, and seize goblins with telekinesis to fling them into the walls of a ruined arcane sanctuary. Fight down through three floors, each with its own guardian, and choose boons that change how your spells behave.
