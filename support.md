# HEXFALL Support

Need help with HEXFALL? Email **[hexfall.support@gmail.com](mailto:hexfall.support@gmail.com)**. We usually reply within 2–3 days.

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include:

- what you were doing when it happened (for example "Floor 2, fighting the guardian")
- what went wrong (crash, freeze, stuck, a spell not working, …)
- your headset model (Meta Quest 3 or 3S)
- a screenshot or short video if you have one (in the headset: hold the Meta button and press the trigger)

Please do not send passwords, payment details or other personal information.

## Common questions

**The game is too hard or too easy.**
Change the difficulty in **Settings** in the sanctuary (the lobby). Story is gentler, Hard is for veterans.

**I feel uncomfortable while moving.**
In **Settings** you can switch to teleport movement, use snap turning, turn on the comfort vignette, or play seated.

**My progress is gone.**
Progress is saved on your headset. If the save file is damaged, the game restores the last good backup automatically. Uninstalling the game deletes its saves, so reinstalling starts fresh.

**Refunds.**
Purchases are handled by Meta. See Meta's refund policy in the Meta Horizon app or at <https://www.meta.com/help/quest/>.
